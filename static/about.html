About StrideSignal

Building a careful foundation for movement research.

This project brings system testing, data acquisition, and future framework evaluation into one clear, honest prototype.

Our purpose

Make the data legible before making claims from it.

StrideSignal is a prototype for exploring how wearable movement data might support sports-injury research. The current build is deliberately limited to collecting, validating, storing, and reviewing raw sensor readings.

A useful research system needs more than a polished interface. It needs documented inputs, reliable data handling, representative evaluation, and clear boundaries around what its outputs can and cannot say.

i

Research prototype—not medical software

StrideSignal does not diagnose, predict, or prevent injury. It has not been clinically validated and must not be used to make health, training, or return-to-play decisions. Keep this local prototype off public networks and do not enter identifiable health data.
